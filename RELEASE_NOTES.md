# Version 1.0.0 — 11 October 2026

Initial public release of **Exact Zero Detection and Compact Arithmetic Realizations for the Riemann Xi Function**, by Leonard van Hemert.

This release contains the standalone 208-page paper, its LaTeX source, the finite certificate programs and frozen outputs used by the paper, source provenance, reproduction instructions and SHA-256 manifests.

The principal contributions are the exact exceptional-zero index, complete compact arithmetic realizations, the fractional full arithmetic family, adaptive completion, fixed-band zero detection with multiplicity-sensitive cumulative-energy lower bounds, and the jointly capped finite gamma transfer with an explicit primitive-sector remainder.

**The Riemann Hypothesis remains unresolved.** The proved detection and completion theorems do not establish the missing global signed arithmetic upper bound. Numerical certificates concern finite models and approximating heads, with their scopes stated in the paper and certificate index.

The source and compiled manuscript were checked during publication preparation. Verification scope and originality limits are documented in PROVENANCE.md. No external review or priority claim is asserted.

The release includes a versioned PDF, a LaTeX source archive, a certificate archive, a complete source archive and their SHA256SUMS. The linked Zenodo integration is used to archive the release and assign its DOI.
